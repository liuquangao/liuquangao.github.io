I'm **Quangao Liu**, a PhD researcher in Computer Science at the **University of Exeter**, with research interests in robotics and embodied AI.

I explore how robots can connect perception, reasoning, and action. My interests include vision-language-action (VLA) models, robot learning, active perception, and multimodal sensing across RGB-D, thermal, LiDAR, and event cameras. I am particularly interested in bringing learning systems into real-world robot deployment.

## Selected work

- **[3jsVLA](https://github.com/liuquangao/3jsVLA)**: a browser-based robotic manipulation environment for learning the VLA pipeline, from demonstration collection to training lightweight policies.
- **[MSTVI](https://github.com/liuquangao/MSTVI)**: the official implementation of our paper on multivariate time series forecasting, published in *Knowledge-Based Systems* (2025).

I also keep [reading notes](/paper_read/) and a [blog](/posts/).
